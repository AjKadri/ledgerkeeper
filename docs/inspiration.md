Its page does not show a manual review step. *We take:* do not lead with "Sui tax labelling", because Awaken covers that. - **Mercury, not re-checked.** Known for a calm, uncluttered banking UI where numbers are plain and trusted. *We take:* quiet colour, tabular figures, one accent, no decoration that does not carry meaning.
